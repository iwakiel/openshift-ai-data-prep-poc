What happens if this fails?

The validate_retail_data KFP component raises a RuntimeError with the list of failed expectations. Kubeflow Pipelines marks the run as FAILED and does not start Phase 5 (feature engineering). The validation result JSON is saved to poc-reports/ge/run_id=xxx/ in MinIO for investigation. The raw and cleaned data remain in their buckets and can be inspected manually.
